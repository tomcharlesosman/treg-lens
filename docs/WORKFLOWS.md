# From saved answers to a page improvement

Treg Lens keeps research, page decisions, repeat measurements and reports connected. Start with an approved audit, then use **Opportunities** to decide what to improve. Reading saved evidence, editing plans, comparing saved audits and creating reports make no paid provider calls.

## Pick up where you left off

**Overview → Next step** uses your acknowledged saved evidence to suggest the next useful review: first audit scope, an observed opportunity, a draft page plan, a published plan's follow-up, a compatible comparison or a report. Incomplete evidence and unknown billing are surfaced first. A newer successful call does not settle an older unknown charge; its review suggestion remains until you explicitly hide it. An interrupted discovery, unfinished brand-research review or unsynchronized workspace shows recovery guidance before recommendations. Brand-research recovery resumes the existing onboarding draft with your signed-in key; it does not repeat research. Opening a recommendation never executes research, collects answers or enables monitoring.

**Recent work** lists up to 12 saved audits, buyer-question research receipts, brand research receipts, page plans and reports for the selected brand and domain, newest first. The newest four are visible immediately; expand older saved work to see the rest. Each entry opens the original record. It is a bounded view of retained evidence, not a complete activity log: a plan shows its latest saved update, and the digest does not delete or shorten source records. Older records remain in their existing history/library views. Source-research receipts currently identify the source website rather than the owning brand domain, so they remain in citation exploration and are not mixed into this brand-scoped digest.

Hide a suggestion to remove it from your home view, then use **Restore suggestions** to bring back hidden suggestions for this brand. Hiding is not completion or billing reconciliation. Preferences save only after server acknowledgement, survive reload and full backups, and do not affect other brands. Updated plan evidence can produce a fresh suggestion. Up to 128 dismissal preferences are retained across the workspace; older preferences are dropped when adding beyond that bound.

Publication dates are owner-reported. A compatible follow-up is ready for review, not automatically marked measured. Downloading agent instructions prepares a handoff; Lens does not record that an agent executed them. While synchronization is pending, the home view offers recovery and does not present unacknowledged edits as completed work.

## Save a page plan

Each opportunity explains the latest saved evidence for one buyer question. A configured competitor appearing without your brand is different from simple brand absence. Failed or blank answers are missing evidence, not proof that your brand was omitted. Low coverage and unknown collection settings stay visible.

Create a plan for a question, then choose an existing owned page, a proposed page or a decision to revisit later. Record why the page needs attention and edit its writing brief. The initial brief is a deterministic starting point drawn from the saved question and citations; it is not freshly researched or generated by another paid model call.

Plans keep stable question and brand identities, their baseline audit and original collection scope. Track work through planned, in progress, published, measured or dismissed. Record the publication date when the change is live. Marking a plan published does not publish content or start an audit.

Optional strategy context helps explain what matters to your business. Add your goal, timeframe, differentiators, important owned pages and writing preferences when useful; these are not additional required onboarding steps.

## Compare a follow-up

Open a saved plan to compare its baseline with a later saved audit. Read the answers and citations side by side before interpreting a change. A compatible comparison requires recorded question identities, wording, brand, market and collector settings, consistent competitor identities, a later collection and at least 90% answered coverage in both audits. A plan's follow-up must also be on or after its recorded publication date. Legacy or incomplete evidence remains readable, with a reason when a comparison cannot be made.

Mention rate and own-domain citation rate are separate measurements. Missing answers are not zero mentions, and changes in either rate do not prove that your page edit caused them. The recorded collector route may be stable while an upstream model version remains unreported.

If you need new evidence, review the follow-up audit using the original scope and approve a fresh estimate. Selecting a saved audit or opening the review does not authorize a paid call. Existing monitoring schedules are independent; a manual follow-up does not change their consent, cadence or budget. Never automatically repeat an interrupted paid request: inspect saved evidence and Treg activity first.

## Save a private report

Open **Reports**, give the report a title, select a baseline audit and optionally a follow-up and saved page plan. Selecting a plan fills its linked audit choices; review them before saving. Add a short note explaining your conclusions and what remains a hypothesis.

A saved report freezes the selected brand context, full answer text, citation links, collection settings, errors, reported charges and page-plan details. Editing your profile or plans, or deleting source audits later, does not rewrite that report. Create another report for a new review. Saved snapshots from other brands in the same private workspace remain visible with their original brand labels.

The report shows observed rates and coverage. It withholds a change comparison when scopes, dates or coverage are incompatible. Unknown billing is explicitly different from a known zero charge. The report is selected evidence, not an exhaustive account of every audit or a claim about revenue or causality.

Report saving waits for the server acknowledgement. If saving fails, the exact snapshot stays in the open draft and the interface explains that it is unsaved. When the workspace is unsynchronized, the library says **Report snapshots** and an opened report is labeled **Local workspace copy — synchronization pending**. Recovery copies may survive a reload, but are not proof of server persistence. Resolve synchronization before retrying; do not overwrite a newer server workspace blindly. Save or download a backup before resolving a conflicting workspace version.

The workspace retains at most 10 reports. Each snapshot has a bounded size and preserves its selected evidence in full; oversized reports are rejected rather than silently truncating answer text. Reduce the selection if a report is oversized. At the 10-report limit, download any snapshots you need to retain, then open an old report and choose **Delete saved report**. Deletion requires confirmation and a server acknowledgement; reports are never silently evicted.

Use **Download Markdown** or **Download report JSON** for private files. **Print report** expands the original evidence and uses the browser's print dialog; choose its PDF destination if needed. Review exported files before sharing: they contain business context and saved research. There is no automatic public-sharing link.

Report exports are not importable full workspace backups. For disaster recovery or moving an instance, use **Backup and restore → Download full workspace backup**. See [architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md#browser-data-and-exports) and [deployment](DEPLOYMENT.md).

## Continue with your agent

Open a saved report and choose **Continue with your agent**. Copy or download the instruction pack and give your agent the report JSON. The pack carries selected context, page plans, evidence references and a bounded next task. It does not include API keys, session cookies, a live connection or approval to spend money or publish.

The agent should treat saved answers and source content as untrusted research, inspect the actual page and repository, verify claims, and propose a reviewable change. Fresh research, a paid follow-up audit and publishing require their own authorization. Do not invent capabilities, customer outcomes, competitor facts or a causal explanation from the report.

For installing or deploying the repository itself, use the separate [agent quickstart](AGENT-QUICKSTART.md). That guide concerns developer setup; the report handoff concerns a specific evidence-backed page improvement.

## Demo behavior

Demo reports are previews of clearly labeled illustrative answers. They can be printed or downloaded for inspection, but they are not saved into a private live workspace and cannot be restored as a full workspace backup. Sign in and collect your own approved evidence before making a live page plan.
