# Community conduct

Treg Lens welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Help make issues, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces useful and respectful.

- Critique ideas and code with specific, constructive feedback. Assume questions may come from someone learning.
- Respect people's identities, boundaries, time, and accessibility needs.
- Keep private information private. Do not share another person's credentials, personal information, or private work without permission.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, make sexual advances, or use degrading language. Spam and sustained disruption are also unwelcome.

These expectations apply in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful material, close disruptive threads, or restrict participation. They should explain moderation decisions when doing so can protect everyone's privacy and safety.

For a public conduct concern, use a brief, factual issue and avoid personal information. If a concern needs confidential handling, open a generic **Community conduct contact requested** issue without names or details and ask for a private channel. There is currently no dedicated private conduct inbox or guaranteed response time. Do not disclose sensitive details until an appropriate channel has been provided.

For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
